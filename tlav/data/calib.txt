def fibonacci(n):
    """Return the first n Fibonacci numbers."""
    seq = [0, 1]
    while len(seq) < n:
        seq.append(seq[-1] + seq[-2])
    return seq[:n]

The water cycle describes how water moves through the environment. Heat from the sun causes water in oceans, lakes and rivers to evaporate. The vapour rises, cools and condenses into clouds. When droplets in the clouds grow heavy enough they fall as rain, snow or hail. Some of this water flows over the ground into rivers, some soaks into the soil to become groundwater, and plants return a portion to the air through transpiration.
